dwell:refuge - field recording credits
=======================================

Sourced via github.com/Muges/ambientsounds (a free-licensed
ambient sound collection). Original recordings on freesound.org.
All files were excerpted, filtered and re-encoded for this piece.

rain-window.mp3
  "Heavy Rain" by D W
  https://www.freesound.org/people/D%20W/sounds/136971/
  License: CC BY
  Processing: 30s excerpt, 6kHz lowpass, normalized

wind-dry.mp3
  "Wind" by felix.blume
  https://www.freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/139337/
  License: CC0
  Processing: 45s excerpt, normalized

water-pipes.mp3
  "Stream" by mystiscool
  https://www.freesound.org/people/mystiscool/sounds/7138/
  License: CC BY
  Processing: 40s excerpt, bandpassed 150-900Hz to read as
  water moving through pipes, normalized

thunder-1.mp3, thunder-2.mp3, thunder-3.mp3
  "Thunderstorm" by RHumphries
  https://www.freesound.org/people/RHumphries/sounds/2523/
  License: CC BY
  Processing: three ~10s excerpts around the loudest claps,
  faded, normalized

cicadas.mp3
  "Crickets Chirping At Night" by Lisa Redfern
  https://soundbible.com/2083-Crickets-Chirping-At-Night.html
  License: Public Domain
  Sourced via github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket ("Summer night")
  Processing: 45s excerpt, normalized. A night insect chorus
  rather than literal cicadas - the zone's closed-window filter
  makes it read as "insects outside"

splash-1.mp3, splash-2.mp3
  "Boat" by Falcet
  https://freesound.org/people/Falcet/sounds/439365/
  License: CC0
  Sourced via github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket
  Processing: the two strongest water-slosh transients, ~2s each,
  faded and normalized

Attribution note: CC BY requires credit. These authors are also
credited on the piece's entry screen. Keep both when deploying.
